My 2D Platformer Game
This is a 2D side-scrolling game where you run, jump, and shoot enemies across three levels.

Health (Red Bar): Your life.

Armor (Blue Bar): Extra protection. You can collect blue shirts to refill it.

Kills: Counts all enemies you defeat. This number does not reset.

Food: Counts the food bowls you collect in a level. This resets to 0 on each new level. You need to collect more than 3 food items to pass the level.

Level 1: The Forest
<img width="754" height="500" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6edb86a9-427b-4768-971c-3c018ad4c404" />
This is the first level, set in a forest. Here you will fight basic green enemies and learn to collect food and armor. You must collect at least 4 food items to move to the next level.

Level 2: The Jungle Ruins
<img width="751" height="502" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8a0d5fa-f5fe-477e-85e4-1b61849875fe" />
This level is a jungle with old ruins. It's more challenging and introduces new dangers.

Spike Traps: Watch out for the white spikes on the ground! They will hurt you.

New Enemies: This level has different enemies, like mossy creatures and more goblins.

Objective: Your food count resets to 0. You need to collect more than 3 food items again to find the exit.

This is the final level, where you climb up a series of platforms next to a giant waterfall.

This level has big, tough boss enemies. Unlike regular enemies, bosses take 5 shots to defeat and will let out a roar when they are beaten.

You must fight your way to the top and defeat the final boss, who is guarding the purple portal. Once you beat him, you can enter the portal to win the game.
